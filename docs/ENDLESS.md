# The endless world: design notes and state

Branch `claude/endless-world-ce1a5e`, cut from `claude/road-types` (abbce72, PR 2). PR 3.

## Design
- **Countries.** The world is a lattice of countries 10 km square (`TILE`, `src/03_terrain.js`). The home country is the old `WORLD` box (2 x 2 tiles) and is the heart of it. The sea stays the south edge; the land goes on west, east and north. A decision, easy to change in `tileJ` and `drainDir`.
- **Borders.** A border is a belt of high ground along a lattice line that wanders by up to 750 m (`lineOff`, `rimEval`, `rimHeight`): a range in some stretches, wooded hills in others, broad or narrow. Round the home country it is the old mountains, exactly as they were on the home side up to the middle of the range, falling away over 2 km beyond it (`homeFlank`, `HOME_FALL`). Where the old crest stood well inside the home box, a strip of wild land lies between the range and the next country; nothing is planned in it. The player is no longer turned back by the mountains; only the steep-slope rule remains.
- **Drainage** (`drainDir`, `edgeFeat`, `planLandGen` in `src/03_water.js`). Each country is flooded on its own, closed by its border crests. Coastal countries drain to the sea. An inland one drains through one gap to the country south (sometimes west or east; the two north of home go round it, so nothing flows into the home country). At a gap the river is a great river (16 m) on both sides, at a level fixed by the border itself (`edgeFeat().wl`, at least 0.6 m above the next gap downstream), so it crosses without a seam and never runs uphill. Two great rivers entering one country are brought to one level in their first 600 m.
- **Roads.** A border stretch may have a pass (`edgeFeat().pass`). Both countries route a road to the same point on the line (`WaysProto.passRoadGen`). The home country has three: west and east near the coast, north beyond Corrinvale (`placeHomePasses` puts them where few of the old rivers run, and the col spares the ground a river runs over; a col is cut to 17 % of the range's height). The trade over a pass (`trade`) is the same on both sides, so the kind of way matches. A country beyond home has a meeting place near its middle (`ways.centre`), a road in from each pass and up to three tracks out from the middle.
- **One plan per country** (`World` in `src/03_water.js` and `src/03_world.js`): `K.land` (rivers, valleys, landform), `K.ways` (tracks, bridges, grades), `K.settle` (villages, farms, lanes, fields), places in `POI`, and a small map image. `Wild.tracks`, `Wild.rivers`, `Wild.bridges`, `Settle.list` and the like are merged views over the planned countries. A plan is a generator run a few ms a frame (`World.update`, `World.due`) as the player comes within 9 km; it sees only its own country (`World.only`), so it comes out the same in whatever order countries are planned. Countries more than 17 km behind are forgotten (their map image and the places found in them are kept).
- **The home country's plan is unchanged.** It runs at load under `LEGACY = 1` (the land as it was). `tools/digest.py` compares it with a baseline: rivers, tracks, lanes, bridges, villages, farms, field blocks, places and landform are identical for seeds 0, 1 and 2. On the home side of the old crest the ground differs only within 1.7 km of the three passes (0 of some 37,400 sample points a seed elsewhere). One or two river heads a seed that lay beyond the middle of the range are trimmed after the plan is done (`trimHomeRivers`). What changes in the old country: three pass roads, and more high road (42 to about 60 km in seed 0) because trade now runs to the passes.
- **Float precision.** Wild meshes hold their points relative to an origin near them (`localize`, `groupMesh`, `GB.build`), and three.js combines the large offsets in doubles, so no global rebasing is needed. The chunk mask, the coastline texture and the skyline mesh (`genHorizon`, 11 km round the camera) follow the camera.
- **Walking scale.** Hills and valleys beyond home stand at 0.72 of the home scale (`RELIEF_K`); countries are 10 km across (about 43 minutes' walk).

## Tools
- `tools/endless.py 0 1 2`: plans the home country and two rings round it (37 countries) and checks rivers (never uphill, proper ends, seamless at every gap), roads (a road on both sides of every pass, same kind) and the ground across borders. No failures for seeds 0, 1, 2.
- `tools/digest.py OUT.json 0 1 2` and `--diff A B`: the fingerprint of the home country's plan. The baseline of this work is `tools/out/digest_base.json` (gitignored, on the machine that made it).
- `tools/farwalk.py SEED`: flies the player some 110 km through the countries round home at fast-travel speed, slowing as fast travel does, and reports frame work, plan steps, ground not built in time, countries planned and forgotten, and the heap after a collection.
- `SET=beyond tools/wild.py OUT 0 1 2`: eighteen views of the passes, a river gap, villages and borders beyond home, and the home skyline.
- `tools/landmap.py` with `REGION=-30000,-38000,30000,2000,60` draws the home country and the ring round it, with borders, passes and gaps.
- `tools/ev.py "JS"`: boot and evaluate.

## Measured (headless Chrome, software rendering, one machine; compare like with like only)
- **Load**: 8.1-8.6 s against 7.2-7.8 s for the build before this work, measured back to back (pass roads, the kinds of way run once more, the skyline mesh).
- **Chunk building**: unchanged (means 82-137 ms against 78-139 ms).
- **A country's plan**: 0.85-2.1 s of CPU, mean 1.3 s over 36 countries, in steps of 3.5 ms. Longest single step seen on a journey: about 60 ms (river building), most under 25 ms.
- **Journey of 110 km at x40** (`farwalk.py`, seeds 0 and 1, frames not drawn): no errors; 29 countries planned and 25-26 forgotten; ground under the player not built in under 0.1 % of frames; 74-79 % of full speed achieved (travel slows while ground or a country is not ready). The wild's work per frame: mean 20-21 ms, 95th percentile 36-37 ms, worst 90-102 ms. Inside the home country the build before this work does the same 6 km at 88 % of full speed against 75 % now, with the same work per frame (mean 20 ms, worst 51 against 53 ms): planning the countries ahead takes about 4 ms of each frame while it lasts.
- **Memory**: the heap after a collection was 124-126 MB at the start and 202-227 MB out in the countries with 4-6 of them planned. Going 18 km out and back three times it read 222, 224, 225 MB at the far end and 202, 210 MB at the near end: no leak worth the name.

## Not done or not verified
- Nothing was tried on a real device or an iPad. Frame figures above are JavaScript work per frame in headless Chrome; drawing was switched off for the long journeys.
- The game's own fast travel was driven once by road from Pilgrim's Rise through the west pass to a village two countries west (25 km as the crow flies) at x40: it arrived. It stopped once on the way with "Your way is blocked" at a bridge over a great river near its mouth, and went on when resumed; walking the same bridge afterwards, with its ground built, it crossed without stopping. So that stop was the bridge's ground not being built yet at that speed, which the old country can show too.
- Found by the way, in the build before this work as well: fast travel by road from Pilgrim's Rise to the westernmost village of the home country (seed 0) is blocked for good at about (222, -762), just north of the Rise. Not looked into.
- The map was looked at once, west of home. Places found in a country since forgotten are kept by name and position, but this was not exercised.
- On the plan from above the borders beyond home still read as a rough grid of ranges and hill belts. Warping the lattice would hide it; it costs larger planning grids.
- `landAt` is not blended across a border (vegetation may change tone along a border line). No towns beyond home: roads run from pass to pass through villages and a meeting place.
- `tools/wild.py`'s other sets, `tools/invariants.py` and `tools/bench.py` cover the home country only. `invariants.py` passes except `rivers.trench`, which failed before this work too.
- Saves made before this work load, but found places are remembered by name only from now on.
